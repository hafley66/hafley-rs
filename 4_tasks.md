Current policy uses PM3.6 attributes and animations. Common dash duration (15
ticks), stick thresholds (.2/.8), simplified turn/brake behavior, flat ground and
air momentum ordering are explicit local policy. The PM dash-cancel-window
attribute is not used as dash duration. Air A/B remain separate at partial stick.
No exact Melee/PM callback equivalence or connected-network behavior is claimed.
Crouch currently holds the jumpsquat pose. Back-jump selection, crouch animation,
ledges, shields, grabs, additional attacks and a finite playable stage remain
outside this locomotion increment.

Native API: `game_fighter::advance(&mut State, Input, &Rules)` dispatches the
existing Redux Slice. Each tick reads immutable rules and current input, updates
snapshot-owned history/phase/clocks/position/velocity/jump budget, then Falcon
selects its imported pose. Immutable action/pose data is shared outside snapshots.
The existing SQLite boundary publishes derived rows for Godot or wgpu.
