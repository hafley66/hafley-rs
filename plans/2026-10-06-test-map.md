## Folding receipt requirements

The shared runner belongs in `tests/support/`, numbered after its dependencies. Fixture data owns per-case setup. One test and one whole-output snapshot cover each audited concern. The pre-fold assertions/oracles remain the reference until each claim has a named snapshot range; commit bodies list every removed test and its covering lines. A changed result stops that concern without accepting or rewriting its expectations. Each folding commit runs its concern, `t_186_quality_gate` and the module inventory test. The final suite runs once after all fixes on the final commit with `cli,ts-checker` and `hafley_scm/rust-checker`.
